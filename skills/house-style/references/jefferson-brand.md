# Jefferson brand

Use only when the user asks for Jefferson / TJU branding (official pieces, posters, slides for the institution). Otherwise use the house style.

Source: Thomas Jefferson University, Brand at a Glance, March 2025 (jefferson.edu/brand). Check the current guide before print or official use.

## Type

GT Sectra Fine (Book, Medium, Bold) and GT Haptik (Regular, Medium, Bold, Black) for headlines and accents; Museo Sans 300 for headlines and body copy, Museo Sans 700 for headlines, sparingly. These are licensed fonts. Without them, use the house system stack, or Georgia where a serif headline is needed. Do not load web fonts.

## Rules from the guide

- Communications from Jefferson include "Home of Sidney Kimmel Medical College" with the logo or in another prominent location. Materials for individual colleges do not include it.
- Keep the minimum clear space around the logo (half the width of the symbol).
- Never place a department, program, or entity name close enough to the logo to read as part of it.
- Brand elements are line art in Bright Blue on Deep Blue: academic grid, transverse lines, sketch lines, point grid.
- The logo files are not included here. Do not redraw the logo.
